Our Story

About Country Side Beach

Country Side Beach is a Vanuatu-based business rooted in the craftsmanship and communities of our islands. We believe great things happen when local makers are supported and when people — businesses and organisations alike — can come together in a shared, welcoming place.

  • Produces and provides locally made products and goods
  • Supports local Ni-Vanuatu makers, growers and the wider community
  • Provides a physical space for meetings, conferences, workshops and events
  • A place where people, businesses and organisations can come together
